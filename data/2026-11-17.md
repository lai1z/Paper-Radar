Method: 输入是观测（像素环境中的画面），经编码器得到嵌入，训练信号不是让编码器去迎合预测器，而是对嵌入对有向距离 d(x→y)=Σ_i ReLU(y_i−x_i) 施加单一交叉熵损失。相对基线（使用目标网络/方差惩罚/分布惩罚的世界模型），该损失本身就足以避免塌缩，因而编码器不再需要预测器配合；预测器随后在固定嵌入上用普通回归单独拟合。输出是距离有语义、可区分可逆与不可逆转变的嵌入，以及在该嵌入上回归得到的预测器，可继续用于规划。

Result: 作者报告：在四个像素环境上，Sense 学到的 return distance 检测不逆转变的 AUC 为 0.95–1.00，LeWorldModel 为 0.39–0.59；在 LeWorldModel 的导航基准 TwoRoom 上，Sense 规划在 100% 的回合中到达目标，LeWorldModel 为 91%；当目标变远时，所有基线的规划失败率上升，而 Sense 在 Cup、Cliff、Sokoban 中接近零，在 Pendulum 中为所有方法中最低。摘要未报告数值以外的实现细节、统计显著性、计算开销或真实机器人验证。

Conclusion: 若摘要陈述成立，其含义是：隐空间世界模型的塌缩问题可以用一个定义在嵌入有向距离上的交叉熵损失单独解决，并顺带让嵌入距离获得可解释性（可逆/不可逆转变可分），从而在导航规划任务上提升成功率。证据边界：全部结果均在摘要所述的像素环境与 TwoRoom、Cup、Cliff、Sokoban、Pendulum 等基准上，作者未报告真机实验；数值为作者自报，未经独立验证，摘要也未给出与哪些其他基线逐项对比的完整清单。

Abstract: Latent world models predict the future in an embedding space rather than in pixels. An encoder trained only to predict collapses and maps every observation to the same point, so existing models add a mechanism against collapse, such as a target network, a variance penalty or a distribution penalty. These mechanisms keep the embedding from collapsing, but they leave its distances without meaning, and the model cannot tell a reversible transition from an irreversible one. We propose Sense, a single cross-entropy loss over the directed distance $d(x\to y)=\sum_i \mathrm{ReLU}(y_i-x_i)$ that replaces these mechanisms. The loss avoids collapse on its own, and since the encoder no longer needs the predictor, the predictor is fitted afterwards to fixed embeddings by plain regression. On four pixel environments, the return distance learned by Sense detects irreversible transitions with an AUC between 0.95 and 1.00, where LeWorldModel stays between 0.39 and 0.59. On TwoRoom, the navigation benchmark of LeWorldModel, planning with Sense reaches the goal in 100% of episodes against 91% for LeWorldModel. As the goal moves farther away, the planning failure rate of every baseline grows, while that of Sense stays near zero in Cup, Cliff and Sokoban and is the lowest of all methods in Pendulum.
